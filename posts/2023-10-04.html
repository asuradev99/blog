2023-10-04

Today is the day that I failed my first exam (in a subject I care about); namely, 8.012 (Physics 1). So far I seem to be handling it quite well. I currently see it as cause for celebration, as it is a new experience for me. If we're being honest, being challenged in this way is not something I have had much experience with in the past. My IB classes were mostly hard, but for the wrong reason; the work was not hard but there was so much of it that it became challenging by virtue of being incredibly tedious. Of the things that were actually difficult, they were mostly concerned with pandering to vague and obscure rubrics in the humanities.

Now, I have finally encountered a real challenge, the kind of challenge I've been looking for my whole life. Challenge accepted!

A few notes:

  • I should journal more often, but say less. I often feel the need to write long, well thought-out journal entries when that exact combination defeats the purpose of journaling itself.
  • I also want to make a mind-map of all my hobbies and potential areas of exploration. Perhaps LucidChart or Obsidian Canvas are good places to start.

Also, I've been thinking a lot about socializing and having friends. It seems that I possess a few fundamental differences to most other people -- namely, socializing is not a necessary part of my everyday life. Additionally, perhaps it is best for me to have many friends who are not close, then a few close friends, as I think my definition of someone who would count as a close friend is likely impossible to achieve. I will have to accept that.
