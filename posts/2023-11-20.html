2023-11-20

Today, when I went into Barker Library, I heard someone sobbing. At first, I tried to ignore the sobbing, thinking that it was not any of my business so I should not disturb the person in pain. However, the more I heard the sobbing, the more I grew anxious to do something. I couldn't just stand by and ignore them, so, gathering my courage, I went down to check on the crying person. Unfortunately, my anxiety overcame me and all I could muster was a stuttered "Hello", and "Are you alright?" There was no response, and, defeated, I went back to where I was studying.

Again, I was anxious to help out in some way, but I wasn't sure whether I would be creating more trouble by disturbing that person. I consulted this with my mom, who gave me the idea of giving them some food as a gift, which I thought was nice. I bought two croissants from the college cafe and ended up giving it to them, albeit a bit quickly. One of the people crying muttered something, which I'm not sure I heard, and I was too nervous to say anything more so I left.

I realize my attempt to provide sympathy was quite shallow, but it was better than nothing. For today I have shown myself why it is important to try. I think I would have regretted doing nothing a lot more than even the pathetic attempt I actually did. Despite my social anxiety, my worries, my fears of embarrassing myself, I reached out and tried to make someone's day a little better, and that is all I have to be proud of today.
