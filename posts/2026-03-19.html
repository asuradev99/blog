How are Things Going?

Another update. Things are going alright. I am getting a bit stressed about the next oral exam, as the NMR experiment didn't go quite as well as I wanted. But it's alright; I have been meditating a lot more. Today I did a work out, then meditated for 15 mins. I could feel my fear and anxiety evaporating -- it was an incredible experience. Now, I feel empty, calm, relaxed. Working out, meditation and mindfulness does indeed work; I just need to do them more often.

Lately, I have also been thinking about the unity of all things. On the surface, it appears that I am entirely different from a rock. I am "animate", and a rock is "inanimate". But this is a facade -- there is no "rock", and there is no 'I'. There is only the universe, which both that which I call "I" and the rock are part of. Everything is equal, complete, and interconnected. There may perhaps be a pragmatic need for boundaries, but fundamentally there are none.

Also, I had a thought; when we percieve our thoughts, it is a sense, just like smell, taste, touch, sight, etc. In the same way that our senses are fallible, so too is our thoughts. Thus there is no difference in certainty between our sense perception and our "thought" perception -- hence I disagree with Decartes' cogito ergo sum. One might equally doubt the origin of our thoughts as one might doubt the origin of the objects/phenomena we sense around us.
