Today I discovered snails. Not your run-of-the-mill, vegetable-eating garden snails, oh no. I made them. Accidentally of course, as I was never intending to do so. I was fiddling around with me and Grant's force-directed graph simulation and I found that using a different collision detection method (reversing gravity instead of generating forces) when two particles got close to each other made them pair up into groups of two or three and move with a relatively constant speed in a certain direction, looking like a small creature, a snail or a slug. Keep in mind that this was not due to the lack of friction in my universe (the cool-off constant was 0.99), it was simply a property of not calculating collisions correctly and thus violating the conservation of momentum. Funny how simple rules (or deviations from them, as it seems) can lead to fascinating results.
