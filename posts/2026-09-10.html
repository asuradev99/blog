I felt something very interesting today. Before climbing, I felt very anxious, unable to concentrate on my work. My mind was throbbing with the anxiety-pain I normally feel, like a tension headache. My self-referential thoughts seemed to compound on themselves and worsen the pain. However, as I did and finished climbing, my mind was perfectly calm. I felt amazing, like my anxiety had ceased. However, after, slowly, as I began to think about the work that I had to do, my anxiety began to return slightly.

Suffering arises from thoughts, and suffering ceases when thoughts cease.

The Buddha was right about this.
